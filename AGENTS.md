## Current architecture

Delivered by [epic #66](https://github.com/EternalLiquet/bamboobd2/issues/66) (#67–#70) on top of [PR65](https://github.com/EternalLiquet/bamboobd2/pull/65). Details and the exercised/unexercised list: [VEHICLE_STARTUP.md](docs/VEHICLE_STARTUP.md). These paths describe code; they grant no edit, merge or hardware authorization.

- **Generic OBD** (`core/src/main/kotlin/io/github/eternalliquet/bamboobd2/core/`, portable Kotlin/JVM). `protocol/` owns the deny-by-default `RequestPolicy`, framing, catalog and transport interface; `reader/`, `session/` and `live/` own generic reads and bounded serial recording; `model/`, `storage/` and `report/` preserve provenance, sessions and comparisons. No manufacturer knowledge belongs here.
- **Generic vehicle evidence** (`core/.../vehicle/`). `VehicleIdentifier` runs the bounded identification on a fresh connection through `ElmObdReader`: setup and discovery, `0900` once, `0902` once if advertised, 11-bit CAN only, 120 s limit. `Vin` is masked in `toString`, never saved or logged. A VIN supplies a manufacturer code; model year stays unknown because year-rule applicability is not established. It never establishes model, trim, market, modules or command support.
- **Manufacturer services** (`core/.../manufacturer/`). `ManufacturerRegistry.BUNDLED` holds lightweight entries (make, WMIs, `VehicleProfile`s, factory). `resolve` builds a `VehicleContext` from one connection's evidence plus an optional user-confirmed profile, creating the matching service lazily, once per connection. Services hold no transport, and constructing one sends nothing. `CapabilityDefinition` requirements (`Make`, `Profile`, `Module`) and `CapabilityEvaluator` give research-only, needs-confirmation or unavailable, each with a reason. `Module` is never satisfied yet, and manual choice cannot unlock it. Makes live in `manufacturer/porsche/` and `manufacturer/subaru/`.
- **Experiment and offline research.** `core/.../experiment/IdentificationExperiment.kt` keeps the fixed Cayenne F187 experiment and its FAKE/LIVE entry points; its requests stay out of `RequestPolicy` and `ElmObdReader`. It is reachable only from the Porsche capability when the current connection identifies a Porsche and the user confirmed the 92A. `core/.../offline/` analyzes inert recordings and contains no supported Porsche maintenance definitions.
- **Connection owner** (`app/src/main/kotlin/io/github/eternalliquet/bamboobd2/app/connection/VehicleConnection.kt`). Owns the saved scanner (app-private `saved-adapter` preferences), the single `BluetoothClassicTransport`, identification and teardown. Every connect, loss, disconnect, cancel, re-identify or background close starts a new generation; results from an older generation are discarded with its identity, model confirmation and services. One startup attempt per ViewModel; no automatic reconnect or background polling.
- **App wiring and UI.** `AppViewModel.kt` coordinates mutually exclusive operations. Scans and recording borrow the shared transport through a `Lease`; the bench check and experiment close it and open their own connections. Incomplete identification and cancelled/failed scans close the socket before another operation; reconnect is explicit. Preserve cancellation, socket closure, pending-result guards and the persisted experiment power-cycle guard (it blocks reconnecting until acknowledged). `bluetooth/BluetoothClassic.kt` is the paired-device transport. Screens are in `ui/` (`HomeScreen`, `VehicleCard`, `ScannerSetupScreen`, `LiveReadScreen`); app-private storage/export remains explicit.

### Adding a manufacturer or capability

1. Create `core/.../manufacturer/<make>/` with the make, WMIs, profiles, a `ManufacturerEntry` and a `ManufacturerService`; add the entry to `ManufacturerRegistry.BUNDLED`.
2. Declare each feature as a `CapabilityDefinition` with honest requirements and source. Use `Requirement.Module` for anything that depends on a specific module or software variant, and `Implementation.None` with a reason until a definition is verified. Keep categories distinct (for example oil service, intermediate inspection and main inspection).
3. Never put manufacturer requests in `RequestPolicy`, `ElmObdReader`, discovery or scans. A future supported operation needs its own reviewed policy, must run through a `VehicleConnection` lease and check its generation, and needs an explicit user start. Loading a service never authorizes a command.
4. The home screen lists services and capabilities automatically; add a route only for a capability with its own screen.
5. Keep tests lean: `ManufacturerRegistryTest` for selection and gating, `VehicleStartupFlowTest` when the flow or UI changes. Run the core and app checks in `docs/WORKFLOW.md`. Constructed replies and host tests do not establish that any car or module supports a feature.

Unknown identity always falls back to generic scanning. Hardware validation of this flow is still pending; record owner-operated results separately and never present software checks as vehicle validation. Preserve the approval, privacy and safety rules above.

The app limits recording to four selected PIDs, one request at a time and the [provisional bounds](docs/LIVE_RECORDING.md). Support maps are per responder; an absent page, missing reply or unsupported catalog entry stays distinguishable. Saved comparisons retain responder, unit and missing-side uncertainty. Hardware validation of these additions follows the [separate live-test gate](docs/LIVE_TEST_GATE.md). No ECU state-changing write, reset, coding or brake-signal probe is included. The separate fixed identification experiment sends its hypothesis reads only after explicit owner review and Start; software checks are not vehicle validation.
